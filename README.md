**Groundwork 3D** is a next-generation, decentralized impact and volunteer coordination platform designed to bridge the gap between verified non-profit organizations (NGOs) and passionate changemakers worldwide. Built with modern web technologies, spatial 3D interfaces, and real-time field telemetry, Groundwork transforms traditional community service into an engaging, transparent, and verifiable global ecosystem.

At the heart of the platform is an interactive **3D Satellite Globe** powered by WebGL. Non-profits can deploy coordinate-specific mission beacons across the planet—ranging from mangrove restoration in coastal estuaries to disaster relief logistics in flood-impacted regions. Volunteers can orbit, inspect, and teleport directly to these coordinate pins to access live ground telemetry, task briefs, and capacity requirements before joining field teams with a single click.

Groundwork streamlines the entire volunteer journey through an **AI-Powered Skill Matchmaker**, which pairs individuals with active initiatives based on their weekly capacity, core skills, and chosen humanitarian pillars—such as Environmental Conservation, STEM Education, Rural Healthcare, and Emergency Disaster Response. Organizers benefit from an **NGO Command Center**, featuring applicant pipeline review tools, rapid mission creators, and emergency alert broadcast dispatchers.

To ensure accountability and long-term value, every completed mission feeds into the volunteer’s **Decentralized Impact Passport**. Users can inspect their earned 3D holographic honor medals, track verified service hours, and mint cryptographically audited certificates complete with custom audit checksums and verification QR codes. Whether you are an NGO mobilizing an emergency relief roster or an individual looking to dedicate weekend hours to meaningful causes, Groundwork 3D provides the tools, transparency, and infrastructure to build measurable impact together.
